manifest test, and a `pnpm typecheck:scripts` CI step.

- Vendored files are copied **unmodified** (Research "Licence": Apache-2.0 §4(b) is simplest with unmodified files;
  SC1's no-prose rule concerns the generated output under `packages/metadata/standard/**`, not the vendored input).

  them under `Missing upstream:` in VENDOR.md. The generator keeps them whole via overrides (plan 09-04).
- No CI job re-runs the sync (revision 1, D-02: snapshot network steps are manual, never in CI). Reproducibility is
  checked once by the executor (re-sync into a temp dir and diff); integrity in CI is checked offline against the sha256
  manifest the sync script writes into VENDOR.md.
- `overrides.json` is created here with only the `keyPrefix` facts. Its full shape is fixed now so later plans only
  add entries (see Task 3).
- No org is ever consulted. `de-describe` is not used in this phase.
