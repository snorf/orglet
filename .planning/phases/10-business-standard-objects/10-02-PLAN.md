<objective>
BASE2-08: `orglet check` tells referenced from unreferenced unmodelled standard objects. Referenced ones keep today's
warnings unchanged; unreferenced ones (and generator-skipped lookup targets nothing uses) move to one informational line
that never fails `--strict`.

Purpose: SC3 of Phase 10 and the noise reducer for large orgs (backlog 999.6), without touching any existing warning
string, count or the `warnings`/`diagnostics` 1:1 invariant (D-08, D-09; research Q5 side-channel design).
Output: `InfoDiagnostic`, `BuildResult.info`, `Baseline.skippedReferenceTargets`, the classification in `buildOrgSchema`,
the info line in `check()`, tests.

Interpretation to flag in the SUMMARY (research C9): D-08's "printed always" is implemented as "independent of
`--strict` and of whether warnings exist", but the line goes through the quiet-gated `log()` so the existing tests
"prints nothing with --quiet" and "--quiet does not hide warnings ... `expect(logs).toEqual([])`" stay true.
Formula and validation-rule references: a formula or rule can reach another object only through a field with
`referenceTo` (a relationship), so the lookup pass already classifies them; this plan proves it with a test and does not
add a separate formula scan (simplest path that satisfies D-05; flag in the SUMMARY).
</objective>
