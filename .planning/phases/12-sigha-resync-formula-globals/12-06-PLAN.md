<objective>
Resolve the evaluation-time globals `$UserRole` (FORM-03 part, D-18) and `$Setup` on Hierarchy custom settings (FORM-05, D-16),
and keep `$Setup` on List settings and `$Permission` explicitly unsupported with the reason stated.

Documented basis (12-RESEARCH Q2, Q4): Apex Custom Settings Methods `getInstance()` merges "based on the lowest level fields that
are defined in the hierarchy" (user -> profile -> organization, per field); List custom settings "are accessible only through
code"; `$UserRole` returns blank when a field has no value; `$Permission` needs custom permissions orglet does not model.
Orglet choice: when no level has a value the field is blank; the field's `defaultValue` is not consulted at read time.
Output: compile/evaluate support, gated loading in DmlEngine, unit and pglite integration tests.
</objective>
